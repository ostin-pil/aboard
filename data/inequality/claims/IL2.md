Liberalizing residential zoning — by-right multifamily near transit, elimination of parking minima, removal of lot-size minima, and elimination of discretionary review for code-compliant proposals — allows housing supply to respond to demand. In a small but growing set of jurisdictions (Auckland, Minneapolis, Houston historically, the 2024 California ADU rules) the policy has measurably increased supply and slowed price growth at the metro scale.
