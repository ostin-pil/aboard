Concentrated wealth funds the lobbying, think-tank, and campaign-finance infrastructure that shapes tax-policy design. The result is a tax system whose effective rates and structural carve-outs (capital-gains preference, step-up basis, pass-through deductions, foundation rules) favor wealth-holders disproportionately and progressively across decades — a self-reinforcing channel that distinguishes concentrated wealth from a static distributional outcome.
