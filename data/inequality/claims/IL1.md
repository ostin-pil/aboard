An annual or periodic tax on net wealth above a high threshold — at rates calibrated to be below the long-run real return on capital — directly addresses the r > g compounding channel by reducing the after-tax return to capital for the largest holders. Implementation requires solving valuation (for illiquid assets) and avoiding flight (via global coordination), but where adopted credibly it constrains wealth-share growth.
