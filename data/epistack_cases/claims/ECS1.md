Official and popular dietary guidance on eggs has reversed repeatedly over five decades — from mid-century cholesterol limits, to the 2015 US Dietary Guidelines dropping the 300 mg/day dietary-cholesterol cap, to renewed caution after the 2019 JAMA pooled analysis. Each swing is presented to the public as settled science, and the whipsaw erodes trust in nutrition research as a whole.
