The disagreement is resolved more efficiently by better-identified designs than by additional observational cohorts that inherit the same confounding structure. The highest-leverage moves are individual-participant-data reanalysis with harmonized confounder adjustment across cohorts, pre-registered analysis plans that fix the adjustment set in advance, and — where feasible — randomized trials on hard cardiovascular endpoints rather than on serum-lipid surrogates.
