In the general adult population, habitual egg consumption (roughly one per day) meaningfully increases the risk of cardiovascular disease. Large prospective cohorts and meta-analyses reach opposing conclusions on both the direction and magnitude of the association, and any effect is entangled with total dietary-cholesterol intake, overall dietary pattern, and residual confounding — so the proposition is genuinely contested rather than settled in either direction.
