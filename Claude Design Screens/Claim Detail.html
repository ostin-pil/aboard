Platform algorithmic amplification of outrage content

confidence0.55 · domaindemocratic_backsliding · JSON-LD

Statement

Engagement-ranked feeds on dominant social platforms systematically over-weight affectively charged content — moral outrage, identity-threat cues, and out-group derogation — because such signals reliably drive dwell time and reshare velocity. Sustained exposure compounds into measurable shifts in affective polarization, lowering the perceived legitimacy of out-party institutions and raising tolerance for anti-democratic behavior by co-partisans. The mechanism is conditional on ranking opacity: where ranking signals are auditable and adjustable, the effect attenuates. We hold this claim at moderate confidence pending platform-internal evidence.
