# Audit 2026-08 — findings and session plans

The 2026-08-13 audit round, run against `main` at `708c934` (clean tree,
sessions 55 and 56 merged, no open PRs). Method: four parallel read-only
agents over status and roadmap, gaps and debt, security and performance,
and content and distribution. Every finding below was verified at a file
and line on that commit, not carried forward from an earlier sweep; the
two facts the agents disagreed on (earliest resolution date, README table)
were re-checked by hand before this document was written.

Relationship to `code-quality-audit.md`: that file stays the tracker for
the A–E findings and their status, and nothing here restates its rows.
This document records what the 2026-08 round found beyond them — five new
families (S security, P performance, U untracked debt, M distribution,
R records) — and then cuts the actionable backlog, carried E-items
included, into nine session-sized chunks with a plan each. A session that
takes a chunk closes its E-numbered items in `code-quality-audit.md`'s
status block as usual and its S/P/U/M/R items here.

## Verdict

The artifact is in the best shape it has ever been, and nobody is using
it. Engineering quality is high: a ten-command gate, 584 tests, every
E1–E9 finding closed, the graph component split into tested pure modules.
The diagnosis the project wrote about itself on 2026-07-22 ("the binding
constraint has flipped from engineering to distribution",
`research/reflection-2026-07.md`) has widened since: sessions 40–56 were
almost entirely refactors and gate ratchets while the distribution
backlog was copied verbatim into every log and never picked up. Total
measurable external consumption is zero (Smithery `useCount: 0` is the
only hard number in existence), and there is no instrumentation that
would detect it becoming nonzero. The chunks below are ordered to fix
that: make the front door truthful, add measurement, then promote — with
the reserved engineering items interleaved rather than displaced.
