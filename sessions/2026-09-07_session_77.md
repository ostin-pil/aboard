## Decisions worth remembering

- **`plans/` is the record of what is open, not of what shipped.** The
  question carried through sessions 72 to 75 is settled in
  `plans/README.md`: session logs record what happened, a plan's Status
  section is the one part of it expected to stay true, and the closing
  session edits it in the same PR. Stop carrying it.
- **A baseline is a window, not a number.** Session 74's 115 reads were
  right when measured and wrong nine days later because a crawler
  arrived and left in between. The post-launch comparison uses
  seven-day totals and names the 2026-09-05 cliff.
- **Two worktrees, one known identity.** The session-end resolver stops
  on more than one `feature/*` candidate. Here the target was not a
  guess: this branch was created in this conversation and both commits
  on it are this session's, so it was finalized explicitly by name and
  session 76 was left untouched.
