## What happened

### 1. The audit

The four sweeps converged on one sentence: the artifact is in the best
shape it has ever been, and nobody is using it. Engineering is clean (ten
gate commands green, 584 tests, E1–E9 closed, no open PRs), while every
distribution channel that exists is unannounced, unmeasured, or both —
Smithery's `useCount: 0` is the only hard consumption number on record,
and no telemetry exists that would notice it changing. Meanwhile the
front door misstates the project: the README's State table is wrong on
four of five rows, its headline forecast is one the corpus itself marks
superseded, and two docs advertise a `search_claims` tool that does not

The sharpest new technical findings: an unescaped ld+json embed on the
claim and dossier pages (stored-XSS shaped, one-line fix); the homepage

structurally excluding `worker/`, `mcp-server/` and `clients/` from the
test gate; and two hand-maintained MCP tool surfaces with no parity
check. Two facts the agents disagreed on (earliest forecast resolution
date, the README table) were re-verified by hand before anything was
written down.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **New finding families instead of extending E.** The E-numbers carry
  history and a status discipline in `code-quality-audit.md`; grafting
  twenty new findings onto them would have muddled which document owns
  which row. Five fresh families with the tracker split stated in both
  documents keeps every number unambiguous.
- **Agent reports get spot-checked on load-bearing numbers.** Two of the
  four sweeps contradicted each other on the earliest resolution date
  (2027-01-31 vs 2027-12-31). The ladder was re-derived from `data/`
  before the document was written; the wrong value would have distorted
  the funding-milestone framing.
- **`plan_doc` points at the index, not the audit.** `plans/README.md`
  outlives any single plan; when the audit document retires, the key
  stays correct without another manifest edit.
- **Dispositions beat silence.** The audit found three session-38 threads
  that vanished from the lists without a decision. Everything this round
  chose not to act on is written down with the reason, so the next audit
  inherits decisions, not mysteries.
