### 7. Two environment traps, filed

Neither is this session's doing and both cost time, so they are in
`issues.md` rather than only here.

`npm run check:exports` cannot report honestly from a worktree without its own
install. Node resolution walks *up* to the primary checkout's `node_modules`,
which is why `tsc`, `vitest` and `next build` all work; knip does not walk up,
finds a `node_modules` containing only `.cache/jiti` and `.vite/vitest`, and
reports every dependency as unlisted, exiting 1. It exits 0 on `main`. Since
`check:exports` is in `build_commands`, a worktree session would fail its own
gate for a reason unrelated to its changes.

`jsdom` was declared in `package.json` and absent from the shared
`node_modules`, so `focus-trap.test.ts` never started and 21 tests silently
did not run behind a green-looking "563 passed". The count dropping from 584
was the only signal.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **The plan's numbers were checked, not carried.** All five corpus figures
  were right. Two scope claims were not: `search_claims` was in three docs
  rather than two, and `agent-onboarding.md` had two more defects the audit
  had not named. The habit is cheap and paid twice in one session.
- **F7 is a better headline than F4 on the merits, not just because F4 is
  retired.** Widest live spread, and the dissent is an argued disagreement
  about method rather than a miscalibrated model. Swapping in the
  next-best-numbered live forecast would have missed that.
- **The pure module is what made the component testable.** Putting the graph
  lookup inside `SupersededNotice.tsx` would have been fewer files and
  untestable under this vitest config, which only sees `.test.ts` over
  loader-free modules. `spread.ts` had already made the argument; this
  follows it rather than re-deciding.
- **Dropped tools keep their reasons.** `search_claims` and `get_schema` are
  named in `agent-onboarding.md` as sketched-and-not-built, with why. Deleting
  them silently would have left the next reader to re-propose them.
- **`gh repo edit` over `gh api -X PATCH`.** Same effect, and a permission
  rule can prefix-match it; `-X PATCH` sits mid-command where prefix matching
  cannot reach.
