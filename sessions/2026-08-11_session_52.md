### 2. The fix is structural, not a fourth sweep

v1, v2 and v3 each appended a layer. Adding a v4 would have restated the same
failure mode: a status section that accretes goes stale in the part nobody
rewrites, and the stale part is indistinguishable from the current part to a
reader who was not there.

So the sweep section is now **one status block, replaced wholesale and never
appended to**, carrying a Closed table, a Still-live table, a moving-the-wrong-
way note and an anchor warning. The A-E bodies keep their original wording as
the historical record, with severity cells marked, so the findings themselves
are still readable as written.

Two things fall out of that choice. Version labels in the marks were dropped
(`CLOSED v3` became `CLOSED`), because a mark tied to a sweep number outlives
the sweep. And where an older annotation still says "v3", it is kept and
explained in the status block rather than rewritten, since another session
wrote it and the attribution should stay honest.

### 3. Precedence, written down

Session 51 corrected a *premise* rather than fixing a defect, which the document
had no way to express: the v2 delta list still asserted "no deploy automation"
several screens above the row that disproves it. The status block now states
that a finding's own row takes precedence over anything said about it elsewhere,
and the v2 bullet carries an inline pointer to E10's row. The v2 text itself was
left intact, because it is the record of what v2 believed.

### 4. Also corrected

The C-section's cheap-gates list described five shipped gates as pending, and
the sequencing section still called batch 3 upcoming and E10 "the largest
single piece of unfixed write-path risk". Both updated, and the sequencing now
carries the two gaps later sessions named that the original audit never had:
`code_globs` has no reader for config (`ci.yml`, `wrangler.jsonc`), and a stale
edge id fails at CI rather than at submit.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **A status section that accretes will go stale in the layer nobody rewrites.**
  Three sweeps produced three layers, and the older two were wrong within days.
  Rewriting one block in place is the only version of this that stays true.
- **Read the row before summarising the row.** The first draft filed E10 as
  closed on the strength of a session log's headline. Its own row said partly
  closed and still live, which is the accurate reading, and the two would have
  contradicted each other in the same document.
- **A correction to a premise needs somewhere to live.** Fixing a defect has an
  obvious home; discovering the defect was never real does not. Stating that a
  finding's row outranks every summary of it gives those corrections a place.
- **Keep another session's label, explain it, do not rewrite it.** The "v3"
  annotations stay because someone else wrote them; a note in the status block
  is cheaper than rewriting history and more honest than erasing it.
- **The document that chooses the work is worth more upkeep than the work.**
  This cost one session. Every session that reads a stale audit risks re-fixing
  something already fixed, and two of them nearly did.
